    The road to the platinum

    Five stages, in the order the Atlas recommends. Story first — the missable trophies live there — then activities, then the map, then crime, then money and cleanup. Each stage lists its trophies with the ones you have earned already ticked.

    Missable trophies — do these first

    Ten trophies name a single moment rather than a grind you can return to: a story challenge, a partner beside you, a one-off event. Each card says why it is flagged, in the guide’s own words. Play them before the moment passes — a manual save before each one costs nothing.

    About this Atlas

    Vice Trophy Atlas is an offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. It runs entirely from its own files — no network, no account, no tracking.

    How to use it

    • Checklist: tick a trophy off when you earn it; progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and survives reloads.
    • Export / Import: export your progress to a JSON file and import it back on another machine — or keep it as a backup.
    • Filters: narrow the list by kind, grade, earned or missing, missable only, or free-text search.
    • Spoiler switch: with spoilers hidden, story trophies stay unnamed until you earn them (or turn the switch off).
    • Roadmap: the five stages to the platinum, in order, with per-stage progress.
    • Missables: the ten one-shot trophies, each with the reason it cannot wait.

    Sources

    Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles (the two agree on every name, description and grade); progress targets (counts) come from PSNProfiles. Every guide is written in the Atlas’s own words and cites its sources beside each trophy. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or screenshot appears anywhere in this guide.
