      About this guide

      Leonida Ledger is a free, open-source, offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files: no account, no network, no ads. Your progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your computer unless you export it.

      Where every fact comes from

      • Official trophy list (Exophase) — every trophy's name, description and grade, as PlayStation publishes them. exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/
      • PSNProfiles trophy list — PSN's progress counts wherever this guide gives a number (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties, 9 activity types, 500 choices). psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi
      • Our own advice — every guide is written for this entry from the official description alone. The game launched on 2026-11-19, so a step that goes beyond the description is labelled as not yet checked in the game. No location or trick is claimed as verified where the description does not say it.

      How the flags are built

      Missable means the official description names one moment, one person or one choice — a partner beside you at 4 stars, a rival's single challenge, a unique animal, a second try after a first failure — rather than a grind you can return to. Spoiler means a story or named-mission trophy: with “Hide story spoilers” on, its name and description stay hidden until you earn it. There are no online trophies in the official list, so there is no online filter — everything here is earned in the single-player game.

      Offline & open

      Every file this guide loads lives under its own folder. The data file is data/trophies.json; your progress lives in localStorage under the key gta6-trophy-progress-v1. Export writes a JSON file you can keep or move to another browser; import reads that same file back.
